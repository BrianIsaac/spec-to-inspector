# asus-ugen-hack

An entry for the ASUS UGen AI League Innovation Hackathon (ASUS with Intel
and Hailo), 2026. Contest page: https://contest.bhuntr.com/en/39jg9vimiynrhlksze/home/

Stage I (proposal deck of up to 20 pages, a demo video of up to 3 minutes
on YouTube, a GitHub link) is due 14 October 2026, 17:00 Taipei. Two tracks:
Battlefield Lightning (ASUS UGen300, Hailo-10H) and Battlefield Thunderstorm
(ASUS UGen Intel Arc Pro B70 32GB, through the organiser's VM). Three
themes: Creator AI, Workplace AI, Everyday AI. Global Online format (no
travel) unless decided otherwise.

The entry must be new work: nothing previously published, entered or
awarded. Winners grant the organisers a perpetual royalty-free commercial
licence to the work.

Research findings go to `docs/research/`, briefs to `docs/research/briefs/`,
plans to `docs/plans/`. All of `docs/` is tracked in this repository.

## Working rules

- British spelling. No em dash. No emojis. Conventional commits, small and
  atomic, no attribution of any kind in a commit.
- A source older than about six months is not evidence for a current fact;
  every source carries its URL and date. Unknown is an answer.
- Nothing is pushed: there is no remote.
